# my_data — your own project's files

Everything you add here is yours. It is **not** part of the workshop's files, and it is never
uploaded anywhere by the workflow.

| File | What it is | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `my_eelgrass_carbon.xlsx` | Your digital data sheet: field data sheet and lab results together | `Start_Here.R` copies the blank sheet here the first time; you fill it in |
| `boundary.csv` | Your reporting area for Option B: columns `longitude`, `latitude` (decimal degrees), one row per vertex | You — e.g. exported from the sampling tool in Part 2 |
| `strata.csv` (optional) | Stratum outlines for the map: columns `stratum`, `longitude`, `latitude` | You |

Then point `settings.R` at them (`WORKBOOK`, `BOUNDARY_FILE`, `STRATA_FILE`).
